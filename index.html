01 / THE EVIDENCE

Little things add up.

We use apps every day without thinking much about what we share. Here are three things that are easy to forget.

Old accounts

You stopped using that app, but did you delete your account?

Searches

Search history can tell a service what you have been looking for.

App permissions

An app may still have access to your camera or location if you allowed it.

02 / ONLINE PRIVACY

You clicked "accept".
Did you read it?

Websites and apps can collect information such as your activity, device details or location, depending on their settings and permissions.

This does not mean someone is watching you personally. It just means it is worth checking what you share online.

03 / YOUR DIGITAL SHADOW

Deleted doesn't always mean gone.

Someone might have taken a screenshot or saved a copy before you deleted a post. Some services may also keep certain records for a period of time.

Think before sharing. Every now and then, check your old accounts and app permissions.

FINAL FILE

The case was about you.

Not because a stranger is secretly following you. Your digital footprint is simply the trail you leave while using the internet.
